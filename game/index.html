VICE SHORES

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

Welcome to Vice Shores — a sun-bleached coastal city where every parked car is an opportunity and every siren is a warning. Steal cars, outrun the police, and run four story missions from a hot-wired joyride to the big bank score.

WASD — walk / drive  ·  E — enter a car, grab a package, crack the vault (hold)
Click — shoot (aim with the mouse)  ·  R — reload  ·  Space — brake
Shift — sprint  ·  P — pause  ·  M — mute  ·  drag mouse to look around
Stars rise when you steal, shoot or hit pedestrians. At 3+ stars, officers on foot will try to arrest you. Health and armour pickups are scattered around the city. Progress saves automatically.

Plays fully offline in your browser. No account, no download, no network.
